# Cardamom and ginger tea
This guide helps you to prepare cardamom and ginger tea.

## Prerequisites
Before you start, make sure that you have:

- A gas stove or induction stove
- A tea pan (500 ml or larger) with a heat-resistant handle
- A spoon
- A small knife to peel the ginger
- A mortar and pestle to crush the ginger and cardamom
- A tea strainer
- Two cups or mugs for serving

## Ingredients
   * Fresh water (2 cups)
   * Cardamom (1 pod, slightly crushed)
   * Ginger (1 tsp, fresh ginger, peeled and crushed)
   * Black tea powder (1 tsp)
   * Sugar (2 tsp)
   * Milk (1/2 cup)

## Steps:
   1. Pour two cups of fresh water into a tea pan.
   2. Place the tea pan on the stove or induction stove.
> [!CAUTION]
> Make sure the tea pan is placed properly at the center of the stove to prevent spilling.
   4. Turn on the stove.
      * For a gas stove, carefully turn the knob to ignite the flame.
      * For an induction stove, press the power button and select medium heat.
> [!WARNING]
> Keep your hands, clothes, and paper items away from the flame or hot stove surface.

   5. Allow the water to boil for 2-3 minutes.
   6. Add 1 tsp black tea powder into the boiling water.
   7. Leave it for two minutes.
> [!Important]
> Do not boil/steep the tea for more than two minutes. It may turn bitter.
   9. Peel-off the skin of the ginger.
   10. Crush the ginger and cardamom using a mortar and pestle.
> [!Tip]
> Crushing the cardamom rather than grinding it, releases the aroma and keeps the flavour balanced.

> [!Tip]
> Use the fresh ginger to make the tea flavourful.

  9. Add the crushed ginger and cardamom to the tea.
  10. Let the tea boil for two more minutes.
  11. Add:
      - Two tablespoons of sugar
      - 1/2 cup of milk
  12. Stir the tea carefully using a spoon. 
> [!WARNING]
> Do not touch the hot pan except the handle. You may burn your hand.
  13. Boil the tea for another 2 minutes.
> [!CAUTION]
> Stir the tea carefully while boiling. Milk may rise and spill from the pan.

  14. Turn-off the stove.

      - Turn the knob off for a gas stove.
      - Press the power button to switch off the induction stove.
15. Place a strainer over a cup/mug.
16. Pour the tea through the strainer into the cup/mug.

> [!WARNING]
> Pour the tea slowly and carefully. Hot tea may spill and burn your hand.

> [!WARNING]
> Hot tea can burn your mouth. Allow the tea to cool slightly before drinking.
  17. Serve the tea and enjoy.
