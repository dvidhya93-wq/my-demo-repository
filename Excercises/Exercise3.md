# Delete a Scan
## Overview
This guide explains the users how to delete a scan from Scans list.
## Prerequisites
- User must be logged in
- User must have permission to delete scans
## Steps
- Go to the **Dashboard**
- Navigate to the **Scans List** 
- Select the required scan
- Click **Delete**
- Confirm **Yes** when prompted
## Important Notes
Once the scan is deleted, it cannot be recovered.

## Trobleshooting
### Delete may fail due to permission issue. 
- Contact your administrator for necessary permissions.
