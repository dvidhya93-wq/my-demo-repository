# Delete a Scan

## Overview
This guide explains how to delete a scan from the scans list.

## Prerequisites
- User must be logged in

## Steps
1. Go to the **Dashboard**
2. Navigate to the **Scans List**
3. Select the required scan
4. Click **Delete**
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

## Important Notes
- Confirmation is required before deletion
- Deleted scans cannot be recovered

## Troubleshooting

### Permission Issue
- Deletion may fail due to insufficient permissions
- Contact the administrator for access
