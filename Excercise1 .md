# Scan with Appscan

## Overview
The users can scan the application. 

## Prerequisites
Go to the **Appscan website** and download the Appscan.

## Install Appscan
1. Open the **Appscan.exe** file. Installation wizard opens.
1. Click **Next**.
2. Click **Finish**.
The Appscan is installed on your device.

## Appscan Login 
1. Open **Appscan**.
2. Enter **Email and Password**.
Appscan is ready to use.

## Scan using Appscan
1. Open **Appscan**.
2. Click **New Scan**.

## Troubleshooting
When scan fails,
1. Go to **Dashboard**.
2. Retry **Scan**.

## Error Codes
|Error code|Description|
|----------|------------|
|500|Internal Server down|
