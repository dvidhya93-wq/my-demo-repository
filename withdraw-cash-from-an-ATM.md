# Withdraw cash from an ATM
## Introduction

This guide explains the steps to withdraw cash from an ATM.

[Flowchart of cash withdrawal from an ATM](Withdraw cash from ATM flowchart.jpg)

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure that you have:
- Your debit card
- Your Personal Identification Number (PIN)

## Steps to withdraw cash 

1. Insert your debit card in the card slot.

> [!note]
> Some machines pull the card into the slot. It will eject after the transaction completes or is cancelled. 

2. Select your preferred language from the options displayed.

> [!note]
> The ATM will now display the instructions in your preferred language.

> [!Warning]
> Make sure that no one is nearby when you enter your PIN. Do not share your PIN with anyone. 

3. Enter your 4-digit or 6-digit PIN carefully on the keypad.
4. Select Cash Withdrawal.
5. Choose the Account type:
- Savings Account
- Current Account

6. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.

> [!note]
> It may ask you to choose the denomination (Ex, 100, 200, or 500).

7. Select OK or Enter to confirm the transaction when prompted.

> [!note]
> ATM will now dispense the cash.

> [!Warning]
> Do not leave the ATM until the transaction is complete.

8. Collect the cash from the ATM dispenser and count it.
9. Select ‘Yes’ if you want to make another transaction or select 'No'.
10. Select ‘Yes’, if you need a receipt for your transaction, or select ‘No’.
11. Collect your card before leaving.

## Troubleshooting
|Issue                             |Cause                                                  |Resolution                                     |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
|Invalid PIN message appears       |The entered PIN is incorrect                           |Enter the correct PIN and try again            |
|Card is blocked                   |The maximum number of PIN attempts has been exceeded   |Contact your bank to unblock the card          |
|Insufficient funds message appears|The account balance is lower than the withdrawal amount|Enter a lower amount or use a different account|
|Transaction cannot be completed   |The ATM does not have sufficient cash                  |Use a different ATM                            |
