# Recipe for Cardamom/Ginger Tea
## Materials required:
   * Water (1 cup)
   * Tea powder
   * Sugar
   * Cardamom/Ginger (as preferred)
   * Milk
## Steps:
   - Boil the fresh water in a tea vessel.
   - Add 1 tsp tea powder into the boiling water. Leave it for 1 min.
   - Crush or grind the ginger/cardamon as per your preference.
   - Leave it for 2 mins.
   - Add 2 tbsp sugar and 3/4 cup milk.
   - Use a strainer to filter the tea.
   - Serve it hot and enjoy your tea.
#### Notes:
Do not boil/steep the tea for more than the time specified. It may turn bitter.
